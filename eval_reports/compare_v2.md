## Release decision and acceptance check

`artifacts/panda-router-v2/` is the direct 32M v2 checkpoint exported as
ONNX int8. It is the closest honest release: versus v1 it improves gold
category accuracy (0.660 vs 0.623), gold difficulty MAE (0.877 vs 0.882), and
held-out outcome Spearman (0.100 / 0.108 within-repo vs 0.025 / 0.063), while
the measured batch-1 int8 p50 is 18.21 ms and the 157 MB package is below
300 MB. It narrowly regresses max-utility regret (0.00200 vs 0.00190), so no
candidate satisfies every strict acceptance condition.

The 150M teacher has the best gold and max-utility metrics but measured 55.43
ms p50. The distilled 32M meets the regret and hard MPMC checks (0.00171;
7.2 MPMC and 1.6 HTTP 418) but regresses gold MAE to 0.904. The shipped direct
32M is 6.1 on MPMC (below the 7–10 target) and 1.5 on HTTP 418 (within 1–2).

Outcome anchors improved outcome correlation but harmed gold MAE. The selected
teacher/student recipe therefore used anchor weight 0.3 and regret weight 0;
distillation improved routing regret/outcome correlation for the 32M student,
but hurt gold category and MAE compared with the direct base.

## Limitations

- Gold labels mix 205 Codex+Gemma and 45 Codex+DeepSeek rows.
- The 700 hard tasks are Codex-only.
- SWE-bench outcome offsets are weak, so outcome rho is limited validation.
- Latency was measured on a shared host that was not demonstrably idle; report
  the batch-1 p50/p95 as comparative measurements, not an idle-host SLA.
